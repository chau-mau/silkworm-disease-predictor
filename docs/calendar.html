    Terms & Conditions

    Disease incidence is subject to rearing season, crop stage, and management practices. This calendar indicates climate-based risk only and should be used as a decision-support tool, not a definitive forecast.

    About this calendar

    Each cell is one day of a typical year. The colour shows the predicted risk level from the weather-only Random Forest + Logistic Regression ensemble trained on 2025–2026 field data at CTRTI Ranchi.

    Inputs: daily maximum temperature, minimum temperature, relative humidity, and wind speed.

    Developed by CSB-Central Tasar Research and Training Institute - Ranchi, Jharkhand
