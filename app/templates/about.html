About This Project

Silkworm Disease Prediction System

This web application uses machine learning to predict silkworm disease risk (Virosis and Bacteriosis) from live weather forecast data for Ranchi, Jharkhand.

Research Background

The study was conducted in Ranchi, Jharkhand, India (23.3441°N, 85.3096°E) during October 2025 and August-September 2026. Weather data comes from public-domain sources: NASA POWER and Open-Meteo (ERA5 / national weather models).

Diseases Predicted
  • Virosis - Viral infections affecting silkworms
  • Bacteriosis - Bacterial infections
